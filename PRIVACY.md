# ReadAloud — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 21 August 2026_

ReadAloud is an offline text-to-speech book reader.

## Data collection

ReadAloud collects **no data whatsoever**.

- The app has no internet permission and makes no network connections.
- The books you open, your reading positions, and your settings are stored
  only on your device, and never leave it.
- There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no telemetry, and no
  third-party SDKs that transmit data.

## Data sharing

Nothing is collected, so nothing is shared.

## Data deletion

Removing a book from the library deletes its cached text and reading
position. Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data.

## Speech synthesis

Text-to-speech happens entirely on your device, using either the bundled
neural voice or the voices installed in your device's system text-to-speech
settings.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the developer through the app's
Google Play listing.
